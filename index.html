Admissions: How to Join

We welcome enquiries from parents, guardians and learners interested in joining Kiriani Boys Senior School.

Grade 10 admission

  • Follow the official Ministry of Education senior-school selection and placement process.
  • Select Kiriani Boys Senior School and an eligible subject combination through the official portal.
  • Check your placement results and download the official admission letter and joining instructions.
  • Prepare the documents listed in the official joining instructions.
  • Confirm the reporting date, uniform, boarding requirements and current fee details with the school.

Documents to prepare

  • Official admission or placement letter
  • Junior school assessment results and learner details
  • Birth certificate copy, if requested
  • Medical and other forms specified in the joining instructions
  • Any additional documents officially requested by the school

These are general preparation guidelines, not an official Kiriani joining list. Follow your issued admission letter. For Grade 10 selection and placement, visit the official Ministry portal below.
